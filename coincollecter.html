Coin Collecter

What is "Coin Collecter"?!

Step into a stealthy challenge where your goal is simple—collect all the coins scattered around! These coins have been dropped by unsuspecting guards, but be careful—getting caught might cost you everything. Stay alert, move strategically, and gather every last coin to claim victory! Can you collect them all without being detected?

Download Coin Collecter





Gameplay Overview

Move through the environment, collecting every coin left behind by the guards. There are no shortcuts—grab them all to complete the game! Stay aware of your surroundings, plan your path, and see if you can collect every last coin before time runs out.
